GOOD TO KNOW

Before
we start.

No brief yet? Start with your goal and a little information about your business.

Can a campaign cover just one city?

Yes. ZPR radio offers local, regional and nationwide campaigns. We’ll check station availability and supporting formats for your chosen area.

What budget should I plan for?

The budget depends on reach, campaign duration, formats and asset production. Enter an indicative amount or leave it as ‘To be agreed’. Pricing follows once the brief is clear.

Do I need ready-to-use advertising assets?

No. We can include creative and production requirements in your enquiry. Their scope, timing and costs will be set out in the proposal.

How far ahead should I plan a campaign?

Tell us your intended start date. Lead times depend on media availability and asset preparation; special projects usually need more coordination. The schedule will be confirmed in the proposal.

Can I choose a single channel?

Yes. Cross-media is an option, not a requirement. We may recommend a single channel if it suits your goal.

Who handles the proposal and communication?

Your contact at InnovaPM is Krzysztof Fiedorowicz. I help clarify your requirements and recommendations. Final media terms, availability, delivery partners and responsibilities are confirmed individually.
